Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 12, 2025

1. Introduction

Welcome to the Email Automation Tool ("we," "our," or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our application.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Account Information: Email addresses used for authentication with Gmail and Zoho CRM.
  • OAuth Tokens: We store authentication tokens to access Gmail and Zoho CRM services on your behalf.
  • Email Campaign Data: Contact information including email addresses and first names from your uploaded CSV files.
  • Email Templates: Content of email templates you create or import from Zoho CRM.
  • Usage Data: Information about your use of our application, including email campaign metrics.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information for the following purposes:

  • To provide and maintain our service
  • To authenticate with Gmail and Zoho CRM on your behalf
  • To send emails to your contacts as directed by you
  • To monitor and improve our application's performance
  • To detect and prevent technical issues or unauthorized access

4. Data Storage and Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your personal information:

  • All data is stored in a secure MongoDB database
  • Authentication tokens are securely stored and managed
  • We use secure connections (HTTPS) for all data transfers
  • CSV files containing contact information are stored only for the duration needed to process your email campaigns

5. Third-Party Services

Our application integrates with the following third-party services:

  • Gmail: Used to send emails through your account
  • Zoho CRM: Used to retrieve email templates and contact information

Your use of these services is subject to their respective privacy policies:

  • Gmail (Google): https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Zoho CRM: https://www.zoho.com/privacy.html

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of your personal data
  • Request deletion of your data from our systems
  • Withdraw consent for using your data at any time
  • Request a copy of your data in a structured, machine-readable format

To exercise these rights, please contact us at [your contact email].
